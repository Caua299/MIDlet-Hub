MIDlet Hub is an unofficial fork of J2ME Loader, a J2ME emulator for Android. It runs most 2D and 3D games (including Mascot Capsule 3D ones), with a virtual keyboard, per-app settings and scaling support.

What is new in this fork: Material 3 interface with dynamic colors, game library with cards, Favorites and Recent tabs, a game details sheet, and crash reports saved locally (nothing is sent to any server).

Based on J2ME Loader by Nikita Shakarun, licensed under Apache 2.0.
